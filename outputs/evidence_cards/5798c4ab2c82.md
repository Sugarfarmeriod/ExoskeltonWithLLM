## Abstract
Current hand exoskeleton interaction methods primarily focus on recognizing a limited range of hand motion intentions and rely on pre-programmed control for predefined commands. This paper proposes an embodied interaction control framework based on a multimodal large language model. Speech and image information are fused to infer hand motion intentions and generate motion plans for the exoskeleton, which are executed by an underlying control strategy. Experiments validate the effectiveness and generalizability of the framework.
