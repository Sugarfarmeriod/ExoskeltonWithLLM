Instructions to Authors / Sensors and Materials 1 Sensors and Materials — Instructions to Authors 1. Types of Manuscript Contributions should be specified as research papers, review papers, technical papers, perspectives, or letters. Topics to be covered include physical/mechanical sensors, bio/chemical sensors , materials, related technologies, and sensor applications. 2. Policies Manuscripts submitted to this journal must not have been previously published or be currently under consideration for publication elsewhere. The authors of an accepted manuscript shall agree to transfer the copyright of the manuscript to MYU K.K. All papers published in Sensors and Materials in 2019 and later are available under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 Int ernational License (CC -BY 4.0). Any further distribution of the work in Sensors and Materials must display Sensors and Materials, volume no., page no., and digital object identifier ( DOI). For papers published in Sensors and Materials in 2018 and earlier, the authors of an accepted paper are required to submit a permission request form to the Editorial Department before publishing its PDF file on their own academic website. 3. Review All manuscripts must be reviewed by two reviewers recommended by the Editor(s) (for general contributed manuscripts) or Guest Editor(s) ( for Instructions to Authors / Sensors and Materials 2 manuscripts submitted to special issues). T he final decision (accept/reject) regarding the manuscript is made by the Editor(s) or Guest Editor(s). 4. Fees The authors are obliged to pay the following fees. 4.1 Publication fee The authors are obliged to pay the publication fee determined in accordance with the number of pages published in Sensors and Materials. Withdrawal A manuscript once accepted for publication should not be withdrawn without special reason. If the authors withdraw their manuscript once it has been accepted, the authors must pay the cancellation fee, which will be determined in accordance with when the authors informed us of the cancellation. For details, please contact us. 6. PDF The payment of the publication fee entitles you to a PDF fil e of your paper free of charge. 7. Use of Figures Authors must obtain formal permission from relevant publishers to use figures and tables taken from already published papers. The reference numbers should be appropriately given in the body of the text and in the figure or table caption to clarify the source. Instructions to Authors / Sensors and Materials 4 8.
